## Privacy Policy — minimum scope

**Controller:** `[LEGAL ENTITY / NAME]`

**Contact:** `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`

StudyOS English AI processes account data and learning data to provide personalized English lessons, progress tracking, conversation history, mistakes/reviews, voice features and subscription management.

### Data categories

- Account identifiers: email, authentication identifier and display name.
- Learning data: CEFR level, skill scores, goals, interests, vocabulary, mistakes, learning events and lesson activity.
- Conversation data: messages and metadata saved to provide continuity and personalization.
- Billing metadata: Stripe customer/subscription identifiers and subscription status. StudyOS does not store full card numbers.
- Technical data: request identifiers, security/rate-limit telemetry and service logs.

### Purposes

Data is processed to provide the service, personalize learning, prevent abuse, operate subscriptions, maintain security, troubleshoot failures and comply with legal obligations.

### AI processing

Text and voice inputs may be sent to configured AI providers to provide tutoring, transcription, speech synthesis or realtime conversation. Provider-specific terms and retention practices should be disclosed in the final policy.

### User rights

The product provides authenticated data export and application-data deletion endpoints. Additional rights, retention periods, legal bases and complaint channels must be finalized for the operating jurisdiction.

### Security

StudyOS uses authenticated API access, server-side payment credentials, database access controls, encrypted database connections where configured, rate limiting, security headers and restricted client database access.
